The challenge

Make something that earns while you sleep

Pick a way to make money that does not need your time every day, then build it in a single day with AI doing the heavy lifting. By 7 pm it should exist, work, and be good enough to show to real customers.

This is not only a hackathon. The idea is that you keep going with what you build after the day ends.

  • Anyone can joinNo coding background needed. AI can write the code, the copy and the designs.
  • Solo or teamCome alone, or bring friends and family. Solo builders can find teammates during the ideation session.
  • Use AI freelyUse any AI tools you like for research, building, writing and design.
  • Aim for passiveOnce it is set up, it should be able to earn without you working on it every day.
  • Build on the dayBring ideas with you, but do the building between 12:30 pm and 7:00 pm.

      Questions

      Good to know

      Do I need to know how to code?

      No. AI tools can write the code for you. You bring the idea and the judgement about what people would pay for.

      What if I do not have an idea yet?

      Look at the idea starters above. You also have 45 minutes at the start to plan, and a 30 minute ideation session where everyone shares ideas and helps each other.

      Can I join without a team?

      Yes. Solo is welcome. If you would like teammates, say so in the form and you can team up during the ideation session.

      Can I join remotely?

      Yes. The event is hybrid. Choose online when you sign up and the meeting link will be shared before the day. Coming in person is more fun, and the pizzas are on the house.

      Do I have to stop at 7 pm?

      Building stops at 7 pm so everyone can demo. Carry on afterwards as long as you like. That is the point.
